01 — Practice

Development & Project Management

We turn property opportunities into clear, practical delivery strategies. Beginning with the client’s objectives and the real constraints of the site, we coordinate feasibility, planning, authority approvals, consultant teams, procurement, programme, cost, risk and delivery.

Our development and project management approach is owner minded. We test decisions against the whole project rather than one discipline in isolation. The objective is not simply to keep a process moving, but to make the right decisions at the right time, expose risk early and protect the project’s long term value.

02 — Design

Architecture

Our architectural team translates commercial objectives and human needs into places that are functional, buildable and well resolved. We work from concept design and development applications through consultant coordination, construction documentation and delivery support.

Because our designers work alongside development, project management and construction specialists, ideas are tested early against planning controls, budget, staging, procurement and buildability. The result is design with purpose: clear in intent, responsive to its setting and capable of being delivered.

Architectural Phase Resolution
Concept Design: Yield, spatial efficiency & site response.
DA & Approvals: Planning control alignment & documentation.
Documentation: Construction detailing & consultant coordination.
Site Support: Buildability verification during delivery.
03 — Deliver

Construction and Building

Our construction capability brings planning and design decisions into the physical world. We deliver building and fitout works with close attention to safety, quality, programme, cost and communication.

Early construction input helps resolve buildability, staging and procurement before they become site problems. During delivery, our familiarity with the project’s original objectives supports faster decisions and a stronger connection between the approved design and the completed place.

04 — Integration

How the services work together.

Development is the spine of Projectvision. Architecture, project management and construction are the connected capabilities that bring the vision to life. Clients may engage a single service or an integrated team, depending on the project.

When the disciplines work together, each decision is considered through the full project lifecycle. A design choice can be tested against approvals, cost and buildability. A construction issue can be assessed against the design intent and commercial objective. A development decision can be informed by the people who will document, procure and deliver it.

Single-Point Purpose

This integrated model creates clearer accountability, fewer handover gaps and better informed decisions. Most importantly, it gives the project one consistent purpose from first possibility to finished place.

Meet the Team

Meet the Team.

Projectvision are a project management, design and construction company based in Silverwater, NSW. We have been delivering quality projects since 1999, and have since gained an impressive track record of reliability and client satisfaction.

We specialise in the management of projects ranging from retail and commercial centres to residential and industrial developments, and cover tendering, cost control, authority approvals, project programming as well as construction, design development, tenancy coordination and professional consultations.
